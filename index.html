Before you order

Common questions

Everything people usually ask before buying their first e-bike from us. Still unsure? Message support and we'll reply within a day.

Fat tire bikes (ChamRider, Koolux) use wide 4-inch tread for stability on gravel, sand and snow. Folding models (Victrip, ENGWE) trade some off-road grip for a frame that collapses in seconds, built for lifts, trunks and small apartments.

Every range figure is pedal-assist at a steady 20km/h on flat ground with a 75kg rider. Full-throttle riding, hills and cargo loads will lower it — cargo models like the G-Force DES 2 are tested with 25kg strapped to the rack.

Bikes ship 85% built. Handlebars, pedals, front wheel and the seat post need attaching — about 20 minutes with the included tools. A setup video link is in every box.

Two years on the motor and battery, one year on frame and electronics, from the delivery date. Wear items like tyres and brake pads aren't included.
